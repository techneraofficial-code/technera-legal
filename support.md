## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I reset my password?**
On the login screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the emailed reset link.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes — once installed, all questions and study guide content are available without an internet connection. An internet connection is only needed to sign in and sync your progress.

**How is my progress tracked?**
Every test and practice answer is saved to your account, so your progress carries over across devices and app reinstalls as long as you're signed in with the same account.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
See our [account deletion instructions](./delete-account).

**I found a bug or incorrect question — how do I report it?**
Email us at official@techneraltd.com with as much detail as possible (which question, what screen, what device) and we'll investigate.
